About the project

About

CloudSecure is a B2B SaaS dashboard concept for cloud security operators. It brings infrastructure health, security events and incident response into one place.

This case study keeps the original screens: the overview, the recent events table and the Critical, Warning and Resolved drawers. It adds the story of why they are built this way.

Project goals

Show the most important security information first, and keep deeper detail one click away instead of on another page.

Make severity and the next step unmistakable, and keep the outcome of every event verifiable.

How it was made

AI drew the screens. Decisions made the product.

Every screen in this study was generated with Figma Make AI. That made it fast to see ideas as real interface. It did not decide what the interface should do.

What AI did

  • Generated working interface screens quickly
  • Produced layouts, components and sample data
  • Let me compare ideas as real screens

What I decided

  • The problem, the operator and the incident story
  • What appears first, and what waits for a click
  • The severity language and the action for each state

Review matters. In the generated screens, the overview says “Across 24 nodes” while Live Monitor says all 12 nodes are responding, and the second Warning drawer is titled “Rate Limit Hit” while describing a port scan. Catching that is a designer’s job. These process notes are general; no prompt history is shown.
